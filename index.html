StudyDeck

Put your subjects in order, tick off topics, and time every study session.

How to use StudyDeck
  1. Add subjects. Type a name, pick a color, and press Add subject.
  2. Set your study order. Subject 1 is what you study first. Use the up and down arrows on a card to move it. The numbers update automatically.
  3. Customize. Click a subject's name to rename it. Click its color dot to change the color. Use the cross to delete it.
  4. Build a checklist. Add every topic you need to cover, then tick each one when done. The progress bar fills as you go.
  5. Track time. Press Start when you sit down and Pause when you stop. Starting one subject pauses the others. Reset clears that subject's time.
  6. Your data stays with you. Everything is saved in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data erases it.
